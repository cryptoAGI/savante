sAGI — a verdict you can check.

Give Savante a claim. She reads the evidence and answers with one of four verdicts:

  APPROVE                   the evidence supports it
  APPROVE WITH CONDITIONS   it passes once these numbered, checkable things are done
  REJECT                    the claim outruns its evidence
  DEFER                     this is the operator's decision, not hers

What she cannot verify, she says she cannot — and names the experiment that would settle it.

Try it now: the "Ask Savante" tab. Free, no sign-in, on Bonsai-8B.
Use it in your own work: the /sagi skill in Claude Code (see "dev").
